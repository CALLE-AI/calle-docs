# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and changes, not individuals.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
Report conduct concerns privately to the CALL-E organization maintainers.
